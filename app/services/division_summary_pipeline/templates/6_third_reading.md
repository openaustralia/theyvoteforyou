**Bill Timeline:** *The third reading is the final vote on a bill in the chamber. It comes after the second reading, where the bill is agreed to in principle, and after any stage where its details were examined, so it is a vote on the bill as a whole, including any amendments already made to it. If the third reading is agreed to, the bill has passed this chamber. A bill that started here then goes to the other chamber, which takes it through the same stages. A bill that started in the other chamber is sent back there, and the other chamber is asked to agree to any amendments this chamber made. A bill becomes law only once both chambers have passed it in identical form and the Governor-General has assented to it.*
