No divisions can be held there: if a question cannot be decided on the voices, and any member dissents from the result the Chair announces, it becomes an "unresolved question" and comes back to the House to be settled. When the Federation Chamber has finished with a bill it reports it to the House, and the questions on that report are put immediately, without debate or amendment. That is why a division on a bill debated there happens in the House rather than in the Federation Chamber.*
