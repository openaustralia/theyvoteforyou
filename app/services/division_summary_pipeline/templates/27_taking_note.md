**Jargon Explainer:** *A motion to "take note" is the device that lets a chamber debate something there would otherwise be no question before it about: a tabled document, a committee report, a ministerial statement, or a minister's answers during question time. The vote is on whether to note the item, not on whether the chamber agrees with what is in it.*
