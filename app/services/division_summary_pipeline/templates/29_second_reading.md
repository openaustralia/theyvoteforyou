**Bill Timeline:** *The second reading is the main debate on a bill. It is about what the bill sets out to do and its general principles, rather than its detailed wording. The debate opens with a speech explaining what the bill is for and ends with the chamber deciding the question "That this bill be now read a second time". Agreeing to it means the chamber accepts the bill in principle, but it does not pass the bill. Next comes a stage where the details can be examined and amended (consideration in detail in the House of Representatives, or the committee of the whole in the Senate), although this is sometimes skipped, and then a final vote at the third reading. A bill goes through these stages in each chamber, and becomes law only once both have passed it in identical form and the Governor-General has assented to it.*
