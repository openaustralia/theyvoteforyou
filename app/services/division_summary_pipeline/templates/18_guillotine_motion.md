**Jargon Explainer:** *A limitation of debate, often called a 'guillotine', caps the time available so the remaining stages are put to a vote whether or not debate has finished. In the House of Representatives it takes two questions: a Minister declares the bill urgent and "That the bill be considered urgent" is put immediately without debate, and only then can a motion allotting time be moved. When the time expires the chair puts the remaining questions immediately, including questions on amendments circulated at least two hours beforehand (in the House of Representatives, government amendments), even if nobody has moved them. Anything circulated later can only be considered by leave. Once a time limit is in place the closure ("That the question be now put") cannot be moved for the proceedings it covers, because the timetable has already replaced it.*

*This vote is about how much time the chamber spends on the business, not about the business itself.*
