**Jargon Explainer:** *A motion of dissent challenges a formal procedural ruling by the Speaker or President, and lets the chamber decide whether the Chair read the rules correctly. In the House of Representatives the objection has to be taken at once, put in writing and seconded, and it is then debated immediately. Dissent can only be moved against a ruling. Decisions that are simply the Chair's to make, such as which member gets the call or which matter of public importance is selected, are not rulings and cannot be dissented from.*
