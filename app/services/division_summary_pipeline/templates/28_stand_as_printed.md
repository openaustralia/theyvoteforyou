**Bill Timeline:** *This vote happens during the committee of the whole stage in the Senate, where the bill is worked through in detail. When a senator moves to take a clause or other part out of a bill, the question is deliberately not put as "should this be removed?". It is put the other way round, as "That the clause stand as printed", so the part has to attract majority support to stay in: if the vote is tied the part comes out, whereas the straightforward form of the question would have left a part with no majority sitting in the bill. Amendments of this kind are drafted as "[clause], to be opposed" and are always put separately from other amendments. A vote for the question keeps that part of the bill; a vote against it takes that part out.*
