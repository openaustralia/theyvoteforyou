**Jargon Explainer:** *The Selection of Bills Committee decides which newly introduced bills need to be sent to other committees for deeper investigation and public consultation, and sets the deadline for when those reports are due. The Senate gives effect to its recommendations by adopting the committee's report, and that motion can itself be amended to change the details, or to add or remove bills, so a division here is often about one of those changes rather than about the report as a whole.*
